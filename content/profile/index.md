## How I Work

I build systems by:
1. **Encoding rules** rather than relying on discipline
2. **Documenting through code** (workflows, skills, schemas)
3. **Learning in public** (this site is proof)
4. **Iterating based on retrospectives** (what worked, what didn't)

AI is part of my build pipeline—not a separate tool. Claude reads [[documentation-as-architecture|architectural documentation]], enforces [[claude-skills-pattern|skills-based]] constraints, and self-corrects when deployments fail.
